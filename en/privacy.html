Privacy Policy

EDR-Logger (GPS) / Effective: August 16, 2026 (last updated: November 17, 2026)

This app does not send your recorded locations to the developer. All records are stored only on your device. The developer has no way of knowing where you have been.

1. Information the app records

To keep a record of your day, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Location data (latitude, longitude, accuracy, time, speed, course, altitude)
  • Records built from it (stays, stops, moves, and periods when no location could be recorded)
  • Battery level, barometric pressure, type of motion (walking, in a vehicle, etc.), and step counts. All of these are used to improve recording accuracy and reduce battery use.

About step counts: Step counts are used to tell whether you were walking or being carried by a vehicle during periods when no location could be recorded. They are read from the device's pedometer (Motion & Fitness).

Only if you allow it, the app also receives step-count update notifications from Health. While you are not moving, iPhone may suspend the app to save battery; these updates are used only as a signal that you have started walking again, so the app can wake up. Only step counts are read; no other health data is read, and nothing is written to Health. The app works without this permission (it just notices that you started walking a little later).

About notifications: Only if you allow it, the app shows a notification when recording has stopped because the app was closed. Notifications are created on the device and are never sent anywhere. The app works without this permission (you just won't be told that it was closed).

Only if you also use the developer's companion app “GPEsper” (available in Japan) and turn on instant widget updates there, the app posts a notification that is not shown on screen and removes it immediately. It is used only as a signal to refresh GPEsper's widget and contains no location or records. It is never posted if GPEsper is not installed, and you can turn it off in Settings → [Signal the GPEsper Widget].

The app never collects your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, or payment information.

2. Where the data is stored, and how it is protected

All of the above is stored in a database on your device, and the database is encrypted with AES-256. The key is kept in the device's keychain and never leaves the device.

The app has no developer server of any kind. There is no place where your records are collected.

3. When data leaves the device (always started by you)

Records leave the device or are passed to another app only in the following cases, and only when you take action:

  • Export and sharing: when you export a file (JSON, CSV, GPX, GeoJSON) from the Share Data screen and choose where to share it.
  • Cloud Backup (Premium): when you set up a destination and turn this feature on, newly added records are sent to that destination. The destination is storage you manage yourself, such as Google Drive or iCloud Drive. The developer cannot access its contents.
  • Copies kept on the device: the app keeps a copy of your records in its own Documents folder (Exports). This stays entirely on the device and involves no network connection. You can open it from the Files app. It is not placed anywhere other apps can read automatically.

4. When the app connects to the internet

The app itself connects to the internet in only these three cases:

  • Map background: only when you turn on [Map Background for Tracks] in Settings, to fetch map images from Apple's map service. Your recorded location history is not included in this connection.
  • Buying or restoring Premium: the app connects to Apple (App Store). Payment information is handled by Apple and is not passed to the developer.
  • Backup to Google Drive (Premium): only when you choose Google Drive as the destination and sign in with your Google account.
    • You sign in on Google's own screen. The app never receives your password.
    • The only permissions the app receives from Google are access to the folders and files the app itself creates (drive.file) and the email address of the signed-in account (to show it on screen). The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.
    • Records are placed in the “EDR-Logger” folder in your Google Drive. The developer cannot access its contents.
    • The access token received from Google is stored only in the device's keychain. When you sign out in Settings, it is removed from the device and revoked on Google's side.

Place names for your stays are looked up from data built into the app. No location is sent anywhere to look them up.

5. Advertising and tracking

The app shows no ads. It uses no identifiers for tracking users or devices, and includes no analytics services (including usage analytics and crash collection).

6. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them.

  • You can delete old records in bulk from Settings in the app.
  • Only if you turn on [Delete Backed-Up Records from iPhone] in Cloud Backup, the app automatically deletes from the device detailed tracks (location points) older than two months that it has confirmed reached your destination (off by default).
  • If you delete the app, all records are removed from the device. No copy remains with the developer (none was ever sent).
  • If you used Cloud Backup, the files placed in your destination remain. Delete them from that destination if you no longer need them.

7. Children

The app is not directed at any particular age group and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with its date.
